# Precedent privacy policy

_Last updated: `<publish date>`_

Precedent is an Atlassian Forge app. This policy explains what it reads, what it stores, and where.

## What Precedent reads

- **Confluence pages** in the spaces an admin selects, to find decisions.
- **Jira comments** in the projects an admin selects, to find decisions.
- **Jira issues** that someone views with the Precedent panel open, or that a Rovo or MCP user names, to check them against decisions.
- **Account IDs and active status** of decision owners, and **team membership and goal status** from Atlassian's Teamwork Graph, to keep each decision's owner and assumptions current.

## What Precedent stores

Precedent stores the following in Atlassian's Forge-hosted storage (Forge SQL and Forge key-value storage) for your site:
- decisions;
- their sources (links and short excerpts);
- their assumptions and check results;
- a log of checks, with the account ID or agent name that ran each one;
- admin settings and monthly AI usage counts.

Precedent has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

## AI processing

Precedent uses Forge LLMs, which are hosted by Atlassian, to:
- extract decisions from text;
- judge whether proposed work conflicts with a decision.

Admins can switch AI off per feature and cap monthly usage. The app developer receives no content from your site.

## Retention and deletion

- Data stays until it is dismissed, the demo is reset, or the app is uninstalled.
- Atlassian deletes Forge-hosted app data after uninstallation, according to its data retention policy.
